# Go-to-Market Strategy

## 1. Headline

**Phase 1: Sell the Killian Smart Kit through energizedengines.com starting Q3 2026. Target 1,000 kits in the first 12 months.**

**Phase 2: Use kit telemetry data and customer testimonials to enter rental fleet conversations starting Q4 2026.**

**Phase 3: Launch the full Killian Lift v1 in Q2 2027 and convert one or more rental fleets in 2027.**

The kit comes first because it has the lowest friction, the highest gross margin, and the best demand-validation signal. Every kit is direct evidence of how much the market will pay to fix the pain. That data informs full-lift pricing.

## 2. Sequenced Plan

### Q3 2026: Launch the kit (covered in `launch-plan-90-day.md`)

- 200 unit pilot run
- Direct sale via energizedengines.com Shopify
- Founder pre-order: $599 to first 200 units, $699 retail thereafter
- Outbound sales: 5 phone calls per week to existing energizedengines parts customers who own R-250s

### Q4 2026 to Q1 2027: Scale the kit and start fleet conversations

- 1,000 kits cumulative
- Two kit SKUs added: KSK-S2 (Sumner Series 2000) and KSK-GSLC (Genie SLC family)
- Rental fleet outreach: cold email and warm intro to LGH, Sunbelt, United, Herc material handling buyers
- Goal: one fleet running 50 kits in pilot by end of Q1 2027

### Q2 2027: Launch the full Killian Lift v1

- 50 unit production run
- Direct sale via energizedengines.com to existing kit customers as upgrade
- Rental fleet pilot orders: 100 to 200 units across 1 to 2 fleets

### Q3-Q4 2027: Convert a fleet

- Win a 1,000+ unit annual fleet commitment from Sunbelt or United
- Scale full lift production to 1,000 units / year
- Begin work on Killian Lift v2 (heavier capacity variant) and v3 (smaller, HVAC-focused)

## 3. Customer Segments and Buying Triggers

| Segment | Who | What they buy | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pipefitter contractors (1 to 50 trucks) | Owner-operator or master fitter | Killian Smart Kit (R-250 or R-150) | Adding a battery to their existing lift fixes the worst pain (cranking) for 1/10 the price of a new lift. |
| HVAC mechanical contractors | Service manager | Killian Smart Kit (Series 2000 or Genie) | Same. Plus telemetry to track lift utilization on jobs. |
| Commercial electricians | Foreman or owner | Killian Lift v1 | Lifts a 1,500 lb pull box where Genie SLC is too small. Better than renting a Genie SLA. |
| Rental fleet (national) | Material handling product manager | Both kit and full lift | Differentiated rental rate. Theft recovery. Utilization data. Reduced fleet management overhead. |
| Rental fleet (regional, LGH) | Branch or VP rentals | Full lift | Premium offering vs Genie. Higher day-rate. Better customer experience. |
| Specialty contractors (RTU, glazing, signage) | Owner | Custom configuration of full lift | Killian's modular pendant + telemetry fits their workflow. |

## 4. Messaging Pillars (high level; details in `positioning-and-messaging.md`)

1. **Stop cranking.** The most direct, most concrete value prop. Targets the operator.
2. **Know your load. Know your tilt. Know where your lift is.** Targets the safety officer and the fleet manager.
3. **Built for your battery.** Targets the contractor who already owns 6 batteries.
4. **Drop in. Reverse out.** Targets the buyer who is hesitant to modify a $7,500 lift.

## 5. Sales Channels (details in `sales-channels.md`)

- **Direct online via energizedengines.com Shopify** for kits.
- **Direct rental fleet sales** for full lifts and bulk kit orders.
- **Distributor channel: not in Phase 1 or 2.** Open selectively in Phase 3 if economics justify.
- **Trade shows:** AHR Expo (Jan), World of Concrete (Feb), MCAA (Mar), Mechanical Hot Expo (May).

## 6. Why This Wins

1. **The kit is a Trojan horse for the full lift.** Every kit owner is a future full lift buyer.
2. **Telemetry is a network-effect product.** Every additional unit grows the dashboard's value to fleet customers.
3. **The brand is single-ownership today.** No board, no investors, no committee. Decisions ship in days, not quarters.
4. **The competitive moat is software + data + support, not just hardware.** Hardware can be copied. The cloud dashboard with 1 year of fleet data and a polished mobile app cannot.
5. **The financial model (`financial-model.csv`) shows positive contribution margin from kit unit 1.** No long path to profitability.

## 8. Risks and Counters

| Risk | Counter |
|---|---|
| JET launches a competing kit | The JET JML-X series is a new product. JET does not currently make a retrofit kit. If they do, our 12 month head start and cloud dashboard is the moat. |
| Sumner sues for trademark or patent infringement | The kit does not use Sumner trademarks (we do not say "Roust-A-Bout" outside of compatibility lists, and only with proper attribution). The R-250 is not patent-protected (per `06-ip-and-legal/patent-landscape.md`). |
| DeWalt or Milwaukee complains about battery use | The aftermarket cradle pattern is well-established. We use nominative fair use for trademarks and never imply endorsement. |
| Rental fleet says "we'll wait for our incumbent supplier" | Run a no-CAPEX pilot: ship 50 kits free for 3 months. Their utilization data sells the next 1,000 units. |
| FCC / CE delays | Pre-compliance test in Phase 2 catches issues early. Use pre-certified radio modules for inheritance. |
| Cash flow gap between kit sale and CM payment | Work with CM on Net 60 terms; use kit pre-orders to pre-fund production. |

## 9. Three Ways to Lose

1. We over-engineer the full lift before the kit ships and burn 12 months. Mitigation: kit ships first, no exceptions.
2. We undercharge for the kit and erode full lift price expectations. Mitigation: kit at $699 is correct; do not discount below $599.
3. We waste capital on trade shows or paid ads before the product is real in customers' hands. Mitigation: no paid ads in Phase 1; trade shows starting Q1 2027 only.
