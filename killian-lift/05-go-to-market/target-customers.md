## 2. Segment Profiles

### 2.1 Pipefitter contractor

**Profile:** Local 211 or similar union pipefitter shop with 1 to 50 trucks. Owns 1 to 10 Sumner Roust-A-Bouts. Bought them at $7,500 each, expects 15 year service life. Operators range from 25 year old apprentices to 60 year old foremen.

**Pain:** The R-250 weighs 380 lb, is hand cranked, has no overload feedback, and takes ~100 seconds to lift a 1,500 lb spool to 25 ft. Operators end the day with sore shoulders and bad backs. They ask for a new lift every renewal cycle and the boss says no because the R-250 still works.

**Trigger:** A $699 retrofit kit that turns their existing lift into a battery-powered smart lift. Boss says yes because it preserves the asset.

**Buying behavior:** The owner-operator buys one for himself. If it works, he buys 2 to 5 more in the next quarter. He tells two friends in the union.

**Distribution:** energizedengines.com Shopify, with a discount for Local Union members verified by union number.

**Quoted in:** https://hvac-talk.com/vbb/threads/2213997-Small-material-lifts ; https://www.contractortalk.com/threads/genie-manlift.155826/

### 2.2 HVAC mechanical contractor

**Profile:** Mid-size mechanical contractor (10 to 200 employees) doing service and install work. Owns 5 to 50 lifts of various brands. Service manager makes equipment decisions; owner approves anything > $5k.

**Pain:** Operators lift RTU components (condenser sections, blowers) onto roof curbs. Genie SLA-25 is 650 lb max, often not enough. Operators improvise with multiple trips or rent a crane. Lifts get scratched and lost; service manager spends 2 to 3 hours per week tracking equipment.

**Trigger:** The Killian Smart Kit gives them load awareness and telemetry on existing lifts. Once they see the dashboard, they want a Killian Lift v1 (1,500 lb capacity) for the next big install.

**Buying behavior:** Buys 2 to 5 kits as a trial. Connects them to the dashboard. After 90 days of utilization data, buys 1 to 2 full lifts when an existing lift dies or a new project requires.

**Distribution:** Direct outreach by Killian to top 200 HVAC mechanical contractors in TX, OK, LA, and AZ. Followed by Mechanical Contractors Association of America (MCAA) presence in Q2 2027.

**Quoted in:** https://www.hvac-talk.com/threads/anyone-own-their-own-lift-s.1647271/ ; https://hvac-talk.com/vbb/threads/1008631-Lifting-of-outdoor-unit-onto-roof

### 2.3 Commercial electrician

**Profile:** Electrical contractor doing fit-out, manufacturing plant, or data center work. Lifts heavy bus duct, large fixtures, fabricated assemblies.

**Pain:** A Genie SLC-24 (650 lb) is too small for their largest lifts; renting an SLA-25 is too. They want a 1,500 lb lift but the only option is a Sumner R-250 which is 380 lb to load and 60x60 in deployed (often does not fit).

**Trigger:** The Killian Lift v1: 1,500 lb capacity, 280 lb weight, 31.5 in stowed, 48x52 deployed. Ships natively battery-powered.

**Buying behavior:** Buys 1 full lift when a project requires. Not a repeat buyer at high frequency, but high LTV when adopted.

**Distribution:** Direct sales after Q2 2027 launch. NECA (National Electrical Contractors Association) presence Q3 2027.

### 2.4 National rental fleet

**Profile:** Sunbelt, United, or Herc material-handling business unit. 1,000+ branches, 100k+ units in fleet. Material lift category is a $15M to $50M annual revenue line per fleet.

**Pain:** Genie material lifts are commodity. No differentiation on the rental counter. Theft losses are real ($2k to $5k per stolen lift, ~50 to 200 per year per fleet). No utilization data to optimize fleet placement. Quarterly inspection is a manual process across thousands of units.

**Trigger:** The Killian Lift v1 with telemetry: theft recovery, utilization data, automated inspection reminders, and a differentiated rental product they can charge a premium for. The Killian Smart Kit lets them retrofit existing fleet without scrapping.

**Buying behavior:** 6 to 9 month evaluation. Pilot program with 50 to 200 units. If the pilot meets KPIs, scale to full fleet conversion over 3 to 5 years.

**KPIs that matter:**
- Theft recovery rate (today: <20%, target with Killian: >70%)
- Utilization rate per unit (today: ~35%, target: 45%+)
- Counter return time (today: 8 to 15 minutes for a missing-parts inspection, target: 2 minutes)
- Customer satisfaction scores

**Distribution:** Direct sales by Killian founder + 1 enterprise rep. Initial calls to material handling product managers via LinkedIn and warm intro from rental industry contacts.

### 2.5 Regional or specialty rental fleet

**Profile:** LGH (24 warehouses, focused on rigging and lifting), regional Sunbelt or United branches operating semi-autonomously, specialty fleets like Conlin or Steelhead.

**Pain:** They compete on service and selection, not price. They want a premium product to differentiate from the national chains.

**Trigger:** Same as national fleet but at smaller scale. They will be the first to commit to a pilot because they need the differentiation more.

**Buying behavior:** Buys 50 to 500 units in a single PO. Smaller deal size but faster decision velocity.

**Distribution:** LGH and similar are warm intros via Bez's energizedengines.com network. Rapid pilot agreement, fast scale.

## 3. Buying Personas

### Persona A: Mike, the pipefitter foreman

- 47 years old, Local 211 in Houston. 22 years in the trade.
- Owns 3 R-250s. They're 8 to 12 years old. He runs the shop.
- Has 4 DeWalt FlexVolt batteries already (drill, recip saw, blower, work light).
- Reads Trade publications, watches YouTube videos about new tools, follows a few HVAC YouTubers.
- Decision: $699 is petty cash. He'll buy one to try, install on a Saturday, and decide by the next job whether to buy more.
- **Killian wins Mike with:** drill in a battery, lift 1,500 lb, no cranking, plus the data on his phone.

### Persona B: Sarah, the rental fleet product manager

- 36 years old, MBA. Sunbelt or United. Manages a $30M material lift category.
- Has been pitched by every supplier. Buys mostly Genie because they execute on volume and warranty.
- Has a long-standing complaint about theft and missing parts.
- Decision: 6 to 9 months. Needs a pilot, KPI commitment, and a procurement-friendly contract.
- **Killian wins Sarah with:** 50-unit free pilot, telemetry dashboard, theft recovery KPI, and a price 10% to 20% above Genie SLA on rental day-rate (because the customer experience is better).

### Persona C: Tom, the HVAC service manager

- 52 years old. 20 employees, $4M revenue, family-owned.
- Buys equipment when his current stuff breaks. Hates downtime.
- Has 3 Genie SLAs and 2 Sumner R-150s. Lost two stabilizer pins last year.
- Decision: 2 to 4 weeks. Wants a demo on a real job.
- **Killian wins Tom with:** kit on his existing lift, captive parts on the next full lift purchase, and a phone call that says "I see your lift is at this site, do you need to swap it?"
