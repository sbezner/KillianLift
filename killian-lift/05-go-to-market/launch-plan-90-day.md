## Risks and Contingencies

### Risk: CM cannot ramp to 50 kits/week

- Trigger: Week 4 throughput audit
- Action: Activate backup CM (qualified during pre-launch). Lose 2 weeks but not the quarter.

### Risk: Pre-order conversion < 50%

- Trigger: Day 7 pre-order count below 50
- Action: Personal outreach to top 100 R-250 owners on Bez's contact list. Add a 12-month financing option.

### Risk: First customer reports a critical defect

- Trigger: Any escape past QC
- Action: Halt shipments. Recall affected serial range. Root cause and ship remediation kit. Communicate to all customers personally.

### Risk: Trade press doesn't pick up the story

- Trigger: Week 4, no published article
- Action: Write a long-form blog post on energizedengines.com explaining the pain points and the solution. Use SEO to capture organic search traffic from "Sumner Roust-A-Bout upgrade" queries.

### Risk: One of the rental fleets says no on the pilot offer

- Trigger: Any single rejection
- Action: Move to next on the list. The rejection is rarely "no", usually "not yet"; ask for the timing and conditions that would change.

### Risk: A patent or trademark issue surfaces

- Trigger: Any cease-and-desist
- Action: Engage IP counsel within 24 hours. Most C&Ds in this space are FUD; respond with our FTO analysis (see `06-ip-and-legal/`). If valid, design around within 30 days.

## Success Criteria

90 day launch is a success if:

1. 200+ kits sold at retail (excluding pre-order discount)
2. NPS > 70%, defect rate < 2%
3. 80%+ telemetry attachment rate
4. 1+ rental fleet committed to Q1 2027 pilot
5. CM proven at 50+ kits / week capacity
6. Phase 2 capex secured (self-funded or seed close)

If 4 of 6 criteria hit, the project advances to Phase 2 and full lift design proceeds. If 5 of 6 hit, raise the kit price to $749 in Q1 2027.

If only 1 to 3 criteria hit, pause and reassess. Likely root causes: pricing too high, telemetry value not landing, or product-market fit narrower than expected.
