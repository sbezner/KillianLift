## 7. Trademark Defense

Once registered, we use the marks correctly:

- Register, then place TM (or ® once registered) at first use in each piece of marketing
- Never use the brand as a verb or generic noun
- Police misuse: cease-and-desist on competitive misuse, distributor agreements include trademark guidelines
- Renew registrations every 10 years; file Section 8/9 between years 5-6

## 8. Co-existence with Other Brands We Reference

We reference competitor marks in marketing:

| Mark | How we use it | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| Sumner® / Roust-A-Bout® | "Killian Smart Kit for Sumner Roust-A-Bout R-250" | Nominative fair use, descriptive accuracy |
| Genie® / Superlift® | Comparison charts only | Comparative advertising under Lanham Act |
| DeWalt® / FlexVolt® | "Compatible with DeWalt FlexVolt batteries (sold separately)" | Nominative fair use + disclaimer |
| Milwaukee® / M18™ / RedLithium™ | Same | Same |

Footer disclaimer on every marketing surface that references third-party marks:
